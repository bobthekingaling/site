Quick answers about your everyday power companion, from charging speeds to taking ChargeMate on the move.

01What is ChargeMate?

ChargeMate is a portable power bank with 20,000 mAh capacity, wired fast charging, and a wireless charging surface for compatible devices.

02How many devices can I charge at once?

Charge up to two devices at the same time using the supported charging outputs. Wireless charging is available for compatible devices.

03How fast does it charge?

ChargeMate supports up to 22.5 W wired output and up to 15 W wireless charging. Actual speed depends on your device, cable, and charging method.

04Can I charge my phone wirelessly?

Yes, if your phone supports wireless charging. Place the compatible device on ChargeMate's wireless charging surface. You can also use a compatible wired connection.

05How do I recharge ChargeMate?

Connect ChargeMate to a suitable power source with the included USB-C cable. The power indicators show the remaining battery level.

06Can I take ChargeMate on a flight?

ChargeMate is designed for life on the move, but airline and airport rules for power banks can vary. Check your carrier's current battery and carry-on requirements before travelling.
